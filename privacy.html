Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Your account

Bean asks you to sign in with Sign in with Apple. This gives us:

  • Your name, if you choose to share it.
  • An email address. You can choose Apple's "Hide My Email" so we only see a private relay address.

We never see your Apple Account password.

What Bean saves

Bean saves your recipes, meal log, food notes, and your baby's name and birthday. They are kept on your iPhone and also saved to your account in the cloud, so you can get them back on a new phone.

The cloud copy is stored with Google Firebase, a cloud service. Only your account can read it.

Photos

When you add a recipe from a photo, the text is read on your iPhone using Apple's built-in text recognition. The photo is not uploaded.

Deleting your data

In Bean, go to Account → Delete Account. This deletes your account and everything saved in the cloud straight away. To remove the copy on your iPhone too, delete the Bean app.

You can also email us and we'll delete it for you.

Children

Bean is made for parents and caregivers. It is not meant to be used by children, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Bean changes, for example when we add new features. When we do, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email beanapp.support@gmail.com.
