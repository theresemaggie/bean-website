Common questions

Why do I need to sign in?
Bean saves your recipes and meal log to your own account, so nothing is lost if you change or lose your phone. You sign in with your Apple Account, so there's no new password to remember.
How do I move Bean to a new iPhone?
Install Bean on the new phone and sign in with the same Apple Account. Your recipes and meal log come back automatically.
How do I delete my account?
In Bean, go to Account → Delete Account. This removes everything saved in the cloud.
Is Bean medical advice?
No. Bean helps you keep track of what your baby eats. Always talk to your paediatrician about your baby's health and diet.
